Privacy Policy

Your hours are personal. Here is how OpenHours handles the information that makes them work.

Who operates OpenHours

OpenHours is developed and operated by Daniel Radding. For support or privacy questions, contact support@openhours.fyi.

Your schedules

OpenHours stores your schedule names, days, opening and closing times, and app, category, and website selections on your device. The app and its Screen Time extensions share this information so they can apply your hours and display the blocking screen. OpenHours also stores whether you have completed setup.

Screen Time access

With your permission, OpenHours uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks to restrict your selections outside their open hours. Selections are stored as opaque identifiers supplied by Apple. OpenHours does not read your messages, the content of other apps, or your browsing history.

iCloud backup

When iCloud is available, OpenHours automatically saves a recovery copy in your iCloud account using Apple’s key-value storage. This includes schedule names, days and times, encoded selection identifiers, stored selection labels, and the backup date. Apple handles this transfer and storage. This backup may restore schedules after reinstalling the app; you may need to select your apps again. OpenHours does not operate a server that receives these backups.

Analytics and advertising

OpenHours contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not send your schedules or selections to the developer. The app writes operational errors to Apple’s system log. Apple may process diagnostics and App Store information according to your device settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete schedules in OpenHours and revoke Screen Time access in Settings. Deleting a schedule updates the app’s recovery backup when iCloud syncing succeeds. To remove all schedules from that backup, delete them all while iCloud is available and allow syncing to complete before uninstalling. Uninstalling alone may leave an iCloud recovery copy. Apple controls iCloud availability, syncing, and retention.

Support and policy changes

Contact support@openhours.fyi with support or privacy questions. If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or private app content. This policy will be updated when OpenHours’ data handling changes; the revision date appears below.

Website and email services

This website is hosted with OpenAI Sites, using Cloudflare infrastructure. Requests to the website pass through the hosting providers and include technical information such as your IP address and browser request details. The website contains no advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, or user accounts. Support email is forwarded through Namecheap to a Gmail inbox; those providers process messages to deliver and store them.
