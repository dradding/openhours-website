Pick what needs hours.

Select apps, categories, or websites with Apple’s Screen Time picker.

02 / SCHEDULE

Make room in your day.

Add weekdays, weekends, or your own combination. Set multiple windows, including hours that cross midnight.

03 / CARRY ON

Let your hours repeat.

OpenHours uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks to apply your schedule. You can change your hours whenever you need.
