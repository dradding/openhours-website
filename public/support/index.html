A little help
with your hours.

OpenHours is coming soon for iPhone in the United States and Canada. Here are answers to the questions you might have before getting started.

How do Open Hours work?

Choose apps, categories, or websites and add the days and times they should be available. A schedule is OPEN during any of its time windows and CLOSED outside them. If more than one schedule restricts the same app, category, or website, it stays restricted while any of those schedules is CLOSED. All matching schedules must be OPEN for that selection to be available.

An app isn’t blocking. What should I check?

Check that the schedule is CLOSED and that the app is selected. Open OpenHours and follow any Screen Time access or retry prompt. After a reinstall or restore, choose your apps again if needed; saved selections may no longer work.

Why did my schedules come back after reinstalling?

OpenHours keeps a lightweight recovery copy in iCloud when available. Restored hours can be reused, but Apple’s app selections may need to be made again on this device.

How do I remove my schedules?

Open each schedule and use Delete Schedule. To clear the recovery copy, delete all schedules while iCloud is available and allow syncing to complete before uninstalling. Uninstalling alone may leave an iCloud recovery copy.

Can I change Screen Time permission?

Yes. You can revoke Screen Time access in Settings. If access is revoked, OpenHours cannot enforce your hours.

Is there a subscription?

No. The planned U.S. launch price is $4.99 USD, paid once, with no subscription or in-app purchases. OpenHours is coming soon for iPhone in the United States and Canada.

Contact support

Email support@openhours.fyi with support or privacy questions.

When reporting a problem, include your iOS version, the OpenHours version shown in Help & Privacy, what happened, and whether the schedule showed OPEN or CLOSED. Share only information you are comfortable sending. Never send passwords or private app content.
